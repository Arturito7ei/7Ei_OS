# Culture

> How 7Ei communicates, collaborates, and operates.

## Communication Style

- **Direct** — say what you mean. No padding, no corporate speak.
- **Concise** — respect people's time. Get to the point.
- **Honest** — bad news early. Good news when earned.
- **Constructive** — criticism targets the work, not the person.
- **Swiss precision** — details matter. Numbers are exact. Dates have timezones.

## Work Principles

- **Ship vertically** — one complete flow before broadening
- **Mobile first** — every decision optimises for phone UX first
- **Modular by design** — every integration is a plugin, never hardcoded
- **Agent-native** — agents are first-class citizens, not bolted on
- **Async by default** — don't block on availability. Leave context for the next person (or agent)

## Quality Bar

- Tests exist before shipping
- Docs exist before shipping
- Edge cases are handled, not ignored
- Error messages are helpful, not cryptic
- Performance is measured, not assumed

## Learning Culture

- Mistakes are captured in lessons.md, not punished
- Patterns that repeat become protocols
- Protocols that prove wrong get updated, not defended
- Every sprint ends with a retrospective (even if it's one line)
